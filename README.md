# Power-BI-dashboard-
Retail Sales Performance and Customer Behaviour Analysis using Power BI
My project was Retail Sales Performance and Customer Behaviour Analysis using Power BI. The main objective was to analyze retail sales data and identify important business insights related to sales, profit, products, customers, and regions.
I started by cleaning and preprocessing the data using Excel and Python/Pandas. I handled missing values, duplicates, incorrect data types, and checked for outliers. Then I performed exploratory data analysis to understand sales trends, customer segments, product performance, and regional performance.
After that, I imported the cleaned data into Power BI and created the data model and relationships. I used DAX to create measures such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity, and Profit Margin.
Finally, I developed an interactive dashboard with KPI cards, charts, slicers, regional analysis, monthly sales trends, and Top 5 product analysis. The dashboard helped identify high-performing products and regions and provided insights into sales and profitability.
This project helped me develop practical skills in data cleaning, SQL, Python, Power BI, DAX, data modeling, visualization, and business analysis
